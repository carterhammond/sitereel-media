Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

Why is this cheaper than an agency?

Because production is automated. You're not paying for an agency's billable hours, account managers or office. The shoot is real, the review is human — the editing pipeline just isn't done by hand at $150 an hour.

Do you use AI?

Yes — for editing and first-draft captions. We shoot the footage ourselves and a human approves every post before it goes out. Nothing publishes unreviewed, and nothing gets invented about your business.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. Always. We get access, not ownership. If we part ways you keep everything — accounts, content and the raw footage.

What happens if you're unavailable?

Everything lives in your accounts and a shared folder you can reach without us. There's no hostage situation built into this.

Can we cancel?

Month to month. Thirty days' notice, no exit fee, no minimum term.

Can you use trending sounds?

Partly, and it's worth being straight about this up front. Trending audio on Instagram and TikTok can only be attached inside those apps — nothing posted through a scheduling tool can use it. So we deliver with licensed music by default, and if a specific trend is worth chasing, that post gets published natively instead. It takes about ten seconds.

Do we need to be on TikTok?

Not necessarily — it's part of what we can shoot for if you want it, and most trade businesses have no account at all, which makes it the cheapest reach available to them.

Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

No tracking, no cookies, no advertising pixels, and nothing sold to anyone. This page says exactly what happens to your details when you fill in the form, and what happens to footage we shoot on your site.

Last updated 16/08/2026

The short version

  • The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Nothing tracks you across the internet, and we don't know you visited unless you contact us.
  • The only thing we collect is what you type into the contact form. Nothing is gathered in the background.
  • We never sell or rent your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list you didn't ask for.
  • Footage we shoot for a client belongs to a separate agreement, and nothing featuring an identifiable person goes out without the client approving the post first.
  • Ask us to delete your details and we will, usually the same day.

What the website does not collect

There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising or social media pixels, and no session or local storage anywhere on this site. We do not build a profile of you, and we cannot see who visits.

Like every website, the servers that host these pages keep standard technical access logs, which can include IP addresses. Those logs are created and held by our hosting provider (see section 04), not by us — we don't read them, and we have no way to connect them to you.

03

Why we collect it, and what we do with it

We use what you send us for one purpose: to reply to your enquiry and, if it goes somewhere, to quote and deliver the work.

We do not use it for anything else. Specifically, we do not:

  • sell, rent, or trade your details to anybody, ever;
  • add you to a newsletter or marketing list you didn't ask to join;
  • use your details to target advertising at you;
  • share your enquiry with other businesses as a "lead".

If you become a client, we'll also hold the ordinary business records that come with that — invoices, payment records and the signed service agreement. Some of those we're required by law to keep (see section 06).

05

Filming, footage and people who appear in it

This is the part most privacy policies skip, and it's the part that actually matters for a business like ours.

On a client's site

When we film at a workplace we will often capture images and voices of staff, subcontractors and sometimes members of the public. That's personal information too, so:

  • We film only with the business owner's permission, and only in the areas they've agreed to.
  • We ask the client to tell their team we're coming and what it's for, before the shoot.
  • If someone doesn't want to be filmed, tell us on the day and we'll work around them. No explanation needed.
  • Nothing is published without the client approving that specific post first. There is no auto-publishing.
  • If something ends up in a finished post and you want it taken down, tell us or tell the client, and we'll remove it.

Who owns the footage

Ownership of footage, and what each side is allowed to do with it, is set out in the written service agreement with each client rather than in this policy. If you're a client and you want a copy of that agreement, ask.

Raw footage

Raw and edited footage is stored on our own equipment and backups. It isn't shared with anyone outside the work, and it isn't used to promote SiteReel Media without the client's specific permission.

06

How long we keep things

  • Enquiries that go nowhere — kept for up to 12 months in case you come back, then deleted.
  • Client records — kept for the length of the engagement.
  • Invoices, payment and tax records — kept for five years after the transaction, because Australian tax law requires it.
  • Footage — kept while you're a client and for 12 months after, so we can hand it over or re-cut it. After that it's deleted unless you've asked us to keep it.

You can ask us to delete anything earlier than this, and we will, apart from the records we're legally required to hold on to.

07

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect what we hold: devices are password-protected and encrypted, accounts use multi-factor authentication where it's offered, and access is limited to Carter Hammond — there are no other staff.

We should be straight with you though: no method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. That's true of every business, and anyone who tells you otherwise is overselling.

08

Your choices

At any time you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct it if it's wrong;
  • delete it, subject to the legal retention above;
  • stop contacting you entirely.

Email us and we'll action it, normally the same day and at most within 30 days. We won't charge you for it and we won't ask why.

Where this sits legally. The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) exempts most small businesses with an annual turnover under $3 million, and SiteReel Media is currently one of them. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles anyway, because it's how we'd want our own details handled — and if the business grows past that threshold, the Act will apply and this policy will be updated accordingly.
